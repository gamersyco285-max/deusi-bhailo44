ABOUT MUSHROOM

Understanding Oyster Mushroom Farming

Oyster mushrooms are cultivated edible fungi that can be grown using suitable agricultural substrates under controlled growing conditions.

🍄 Oyster Mushroom

Oyster mushrooms belong to the Pleurotus group and are widely cultivated in Nepal. They are commonly grown on prepared agricultural substrates.

🌾 Growing Medium

Materials such as rice straw and other suitable agricultural residues can be prepared and used as growing substrates for oyster mushroom cultivation.

🧺 Harvesting

When mushroom clusters have developed properly, they can be harvested carefully and prepared for fresh consumption or sale.

CULTIVATION

Basic Mushroom Farming Process

A simplified overview of the major stages involved in oyster mushroom cultivation.

01

Prepare Substrate

Suitable agricultural material such as straw is prepared, cleaned and treated before being used as a growing medium.

02

Add Spawn

Mushroom spawn is introduced into the prepared substrate using clean cultivation practices.

03

Incubation

The growing bags are kept under appropriate conditions while the mushroom mycelium colonizes the substrate.

04

Fruiting & Harvest

Once the bags are ready for fruiting, suitable humidity, ventilation and light conditions are maintained until mushrooms develop and can be harvested.
